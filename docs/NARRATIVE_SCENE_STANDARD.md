# Dunya narrative scenes — writing and gameplay contract
Status: proposed production specification, not a religious ruling or gameplay implementation.

## Story promise
A Dunya mission begins with an ordinary person and a real need. The player's choices alter community outcomes through evidence, service, fair negotiation and sustainable institutions. Every NPC has a motive, not a simplistic alignment label. The main industry campaign follows this same formula from a local neighborhood to interconnected cities.

## One scene must contain
- Stable `scene_id`, `mission_id`, `city_pack_id`, `location_id`, `character_variants`, `npcs`, `trigger`, `prerequisites`, `objectives`, `dialogue`, `choices`, `consequences`, `state_changes`, `required_assets`, `accessibility`, `source_claim_ids`, `review_status`, `localization_keys`, `test_cases`.
- Opening hook with ordinary stakes; at least one opportunity to gather facts before an ethical decision.
- Three meaningful constructive choices where feasible; choices must differ in cost, relationship effects, time or benefits, not merely wording.
- At least one safe exit, pause or defer option for optional experiences.
- A repair path when the protagonist spreads a rumor, miscounts supplies or inadvertently creates harm.
- A credible outcome, an ongoing upkeep cost and a continuity link to related systems.
- Character-specific variants for Ahmed and Fatimah that retain equal agency.

## Dialogue example: pantry introduction
**Sara:** "We can use another pair of hands at the pantry, but I don't want to promise people supplies we haven't counted."
- **Listen:** "Walk me through what you have."
- **Ask:** "Can I see the route before committing?"
- **Decline:** "I can't help today, but I can check back later."

**Amina:** "Before we distribute anything, let's compare the delivery list with the shelves."
- **Inventory:** Check for mismatched quantities and request corrections.
- **Access:** Inspect the route for an unobstructed curb ramp.
- **Defer:** Save the current objective without punishment.

## Game state consequences
Completed player actions may change fictional trust, access, supply reliability, inventory, volunteer availability and household resources. Never award faith, sin, divine approval, forgiveness, salvation or conversion metrics.

## Gate
A script with normative Islamic claims needs exact source references, context, relevant scholarly differences and qualified approval before publication. Fictional dialogue and a source URL cannot substitute for religious signoff.
