# Dunya — Additional story modes and sensitive scenarios
Status: **proposed requirements only; no gameplay implementation**.

## Public slander and Islamophobia response
A fictional advocacy organization, journalist, online rumor network or antagonist may circulate a false allegation about the character or community. Players collect verifiable evidence, ask for corrections, support affected neighbors, seek counsel, respond without backbiting, threats or misinformation, and choose de-escalation. Avoid implying a named real organization (including AIPAC) carried out fictional misconduct. If a real group is used in historical educational content, require documented sourced events, attribution and legal review. Source leads: Qur'an 49:6 (verify news), 4:135 (justice), 16:125 (wisdom), 41:34 (respond better).

## Protect a synagogue, church or mosque
Special alert for a threatened house of worship. Priority: call emergency services, warn people, provide accessible evacuation, first aid only within training, shelter and community solidarity. Never turn worshippers into combat targets; no vigilantism or offensive violence. Qur'an 22:40 is a relevant research lead mentioning protection of places of worship; qualified context review mandatory. Include multiple faith communities, non-Muslim NPC agency and trauma-sensitive presentation.

## Extremist recruitment pressure / suspicious approach
A fictional character at a mosque or online may encourage violence, illegal acts or hatred, possibly claiming official or religious authority. Player evaluates the *proposal*, not the person's clothing, ethnicity, worship behavior or supposed employer. Correct options: refuse, disengage, seek trusted community/legal guidance, report credible threats through lawful channels, preserve safety. No mechanic to detect, unmask, evade or profile undercover federal agents. Never assume a Muslim stranger is an informant. Research source lead Qur'an 5:2 (do not cooperate in wrongdoing). Sensitive narrative requires civil-liberties and scholarly review.

## Eid al-Adha and halal butchery educational storyline
Teach intention, animal welfare, lawful procurement, qualified supervision, local regulation, veterinary/food-safety checks, cleanliness, cold chain, food handling, ethical distribution and community meals. Explain Eid al-Adha, qurbani/udhiyah eligibility and school differences using qualified reviewers. No graphic slaughter animations, suffering-based scoring or DIY instructional cutting tutorial. Practical slaughter methods require licensed professionals and jurisdiction-specific food/animal-welfare guidance. Modules: farm welfare audit, supplier credentials, hygiene setup, compliance paperwork, food-safety quiz, package distribution, charitable allocation and waste minimization. Research leads: Qur'an 22:36–37 and Sahih Muslim 1955 (verify context and grading).

## Jumu'ah parking challenge
Traffic and parking puzzle near a mosque: arrive early, find legal space, keep sidewalks/driveways/fire lanes clear, respect neighbors, accessibility spaces and transit alternatives, volunteer traffic coordination with permits, prayer-time management. Wrong parking leads to corrective educational prompt and safe re-routing, not divine punishment. Source leads: Qur'an 62:9 and hadith about removing harm from road (Muslim 1009); verify before release.

## Nikah and marriage
Consenting adult characters, respectful courtship/introductions, family dialogue where appropriate, compatibility, mahr, witnesses, contract, registration, celebration, mutual rights, communication, financial planning and dispute mediation. No forced marriage, coercion, explicit sexual content, harassment or pressure. Separate religious contract requirements by recognized jurisprudential view from local civil law; no universal legal conclusions. Post-marriage story state introduces shared household responsibilities, optional caregiving, finances, relatives and mutual decision-making; obligations are reciprocal and never presented as permission for abuse. Optional parenting arc only with safeguarding review. Source leads: Qur'an 4:4, 4:19, 30:21, 2:187; verify interpretations and applicable fiqh.

## Cross-cutting requirements
- Ahmed and Fatimah both have substantial narrative agency; relevant ritual roles may vary with approved jurisprudence but not general civic/service opportunities.
- No real-world allegations without documented evidence; fictional organizations cannot be thinly disguised claims about real individuals.
- Every storyline must support consent, disengagement, nonviolent alternatives, accessibility and a reviewable outcome.
- Only approved religious and legal text may ship. No divine-reward meters, religious certifications or conversion scoring.
