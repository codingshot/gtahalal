# Islamic content and gameplay policy

Status: proposed governance policy, subject to qualified scholarly review.

1. All objectives are constructive, lawful and nonviolent. No theft, assaults, weapons, alcohol, drugs, gambling, sexual content, corruption, or rewarding harassment.
2. Qur'an and hadith are not decorative achievements. Every attributed claim requires canonical Quran.com/Sunnah.com link, exact source identification, context, translation permissions, authenticity/grade where relevant and reviewer sign-off.
3. Do not portray Allah, prophets, angels or unseen divine judgment. No literal 'blessings', sins, heaven, salvation or spiritual status counters. Track fictional community impact and knowledge instead.
4. Worship animations and educational minigames must be respectful and optional; do not score prayer speed or compare player piety. No in-game monetization of worship.
5. Niyyah is an optional private reflection; no coercion, public display, scoring or claims that a click constitutes religious intention.
6. For differences among recognized Islamic schools, show approved configurable explanations, cite evidence and avoid one-size-fits-all claims. Qualified scholarly panel required for sensitive content.
7. Gender-specific spaces, clothing and role settings require respectful, culturally reviewed design. Never encourage discrimination, coercion, stalking or harassment.
8. Pilgrimage scenes are educational simulations only, not substitutes for rites. No competitive gameplay around Kaaba or sacred spaces.
9. No reproduction of copyrighted Qur'an translations, recitations, mosque interiors, games, branding or assets without appropriate rights.
10. Public contributions remain unpublished until editorial, religious, cultural, accessibility, security and asset provenance gates pass.
