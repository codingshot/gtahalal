## Reviewer packet
For each topic, record canonical reference, exact Arabic/text location, translation attribution and rights, tafsir or hadith chapter, variants, school differences, age suitability, recommended UI copy, uncertainty and qualified reviewer decision. If source cannot be retrieved, mark pending rather than verified.

## Distinguish engineering from fiqh
Prayer time algorithms and qibla bearings can be technically tested, but calculation settings and religious validity remain separate. A GPS fix does not certify a prayer. A timer does not certify salah completion. An avatar movement does not substitute for worship.

## Explicitly unresolved
Local high-latitude prayer calculation conventions, school-specific Asr, tayammum materials and expiry, travel prayer concessions, physical misbaha permissibility differences, recitation and post-prayer dhikr presets. Do not release normative instruction without review.
