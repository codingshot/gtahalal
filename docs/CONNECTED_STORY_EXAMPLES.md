# Dunya — three connected example episodes
Status: fictional design examples; no gameplay logic or scholarly approvals.

## EP-001: The Viral Clip
City: fictional Miami-inspired starter. Characters: Ahmed or Fatimah. Hook: a cropped video falsely suggests a neighborhood food bank discriminated against visitors. An advertiser offers a large sponsorship if the protagonist amplifies outrage.
Choices:
A. Publish immediately — fictional trust drops; correction and apology branch unlock.
B. Interview everyone and verify the full footage — slower, accurate reporting, community dialogue.
C. Decline the media conflict and assist with food distribution — valid peaceful completion.
Twist: the employee who uploaded the clip was trying to document an actual access problem, not deceive anyone. The player must correct the original accessibility problem even if the viral accusation was misleading.
Build objective: transparent community-news portal and accessible food-bank queue.
Connections: Attention Trap -> Narrative Machine -> Broken Food Chain -> street dawah optional.
Research leads: Qur'an 49:6, 4:135; context/reviewer pending.
Acceptance: no accusation against a real group, every choice has a coherent ending, correction available, source claim not auto-approved.

## EP-002: The Eid Supplier
City: Lagos candidate pack. Hook: a supplier offers unusually cheap animals for Eid distribution but will not provide welfare or veterinary records.
Choices:
A. Demand independent documentation and inspection; delay if necessary.
B. Source from a verified cooperative and adjust budget.
C. Focus on food parcels from an approved partner while professional reviewers assess animal sourcing.
Twist: a trusted vendor made a paperwork error rather than intentionally abusing animals; fair process matters.
Build objective: traceable halal food cooperative with qualified oversight.
Connections: Food Chain -> Eid education -> family food security -> ethical trade.
Research leads: Qur'an 22:36–37; Muslim 1955 pending direct verification; professional and scholarly review required.
Acceptance: no graphic slaughter or DIY cutting tutorial, no unsupported certification, animal welfare and food safety are mandatory.

## EP-003: The Family Ledger
City: Nairobi candidate pack. Hook: after an optional family bereavement chapter, relatives discover conflicting land records and an unpaid debt. A property broker pressures the family to sell immediately.
Choices:
A. Verify title, liabilities, heir status and applicable law with independent experts.
B. Request mediation and temporary protection of dependents while documents are reviewed.
C. Decline direct estate management and appoint qualified, accountable representatives.
Twist: an apparent inheritance dispute is partly caused by a prior informal loan; no character is automatically a villain.
Build objective: transparent estate-administration service and community legal-literacy program.
Connections: Generations -> Janazah (skippable) -> estate -> housing -> next-generation legacy.
Research leads: Qur'an 4:7, 4:11–12, 4:176; faraid scholar and local probate lawyer review pending.
Acceptance: no automatic percentages or asset transfer; surviving spouse and minors retain agency; no points for death.
