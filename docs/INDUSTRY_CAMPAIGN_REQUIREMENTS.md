# Industry campaign specification — content only

## Industry chapter schema
id, slug, cityPackIds, protagonistArcs, antagonistFictionalEntity, socialProblem, stakeholders, competingExplanations, evidenceClaims, ethicalDilemmas, questBeats, institutionToBuild, resourceConstraints, reviewGate, communityOutcomes, unintendedConsequences, sourceRefs, legalReview, accessibility, ageRating, chapterUnlocks, stateContinuity, tests, status.

## Progression
City exploration -> identify harm -> verify evidence -> listen to affected communities -> build ethical alternative -> pilot -> transparent feedback -> sustainable scale -> independent audit. No forced scripted conversions or 'defeat all opponents' combat. Multiple legitimate nonviolent paths to completion, including cooperation with incumbents who reform.

## Infrastructure-building mechanics (future)
- Social platform: moderation policy, non-addictive ranking, privacy-first federation, creator economics, consent and data portability.
- Media: source verification, editorial independence, corrections, conflict-of-interest disclosure, media literacy.
- Food: humane animal welfare, licensed halal oversight, food safety, transparent suppliers, distribution and waste.
- Finance: Sharia governance, consumer protection, risk, financial literacy and regulatory compliance.
- Intelligence/privacy: local inference, encryption, data minimization, threat modeling, transparency and lawful oversight; no espionage, surveillance evasion or cyberattack tutorials.
- Trade: transparent procurement, supply-chain labor conditions, logistics, import/export compliance, open standards.
- Housing: affordability, accessibility, zoning, tenancy and neighborhood trust.
- Education: teachers, open curriculum, source verification, safeguarding and access.
- Healthcare: evidence, clinical oversight, privacy, procurement and equitable care.
- Civic reform: petitions, hearings, non-discriminatory legislation, anti-corruption and procedural fairness.

## Branching ethics examples
A platform founder is offered addictive growth hacks; can reject, transparently test healthier defaults or document trade-offs. A journalist receives an unverified leak; must verify before publishing. A finance co-op faces pressure to misrepresent terms; must disclose and redesign. A privacy startup can collect extra data for convenience; must explain consent and alternatives. A halal supplier may have valid documentation but welfare concerns; seek inspection, avoid public defamation.

## Acceptance
Every chapter has 3+ meaningful peaceful choices, a verified-or-pending source ledger, local context review, no defamatory real-world analog, at least one accessible alternate route, sustainability trade-offs and consequences for bad decisions without divine-score mechanics. No implementation before story and evidence approval.
