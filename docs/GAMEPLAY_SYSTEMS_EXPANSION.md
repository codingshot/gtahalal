# Dunya — Dawah, travel, civic service and learning systems
Status: product specifications only. No feature described here is implemented.

## Street dawah
NPC consent-first conversations at approved public locations; permit check based on fictional local rules, folding table and information stand, free Qur'an translations with licensed text, literature inventory, respectful Q&A, listening and dialogue, accessibility, weather, safe public-space clearance. Player may end conversations without penalty. No conversion quota, coercion, pressure on children, harassment, obstructing pedestrians or religious superiority scoring. Quest outcomes: mutual understanding and respectful conduct, not real-world conversion metrics.

## Civic engagement and community reform
Peaceful community consultation, transparent petitions, local public hearings, drafting proposals, coalition building, lawful organizing, litter and road safety projects, accessible infrastructure advocacy. NPCs may disagree; no automatic approval, threats, property damage, intimidation or targeted harassment. Campaigns to prohibit a harmful commercial practice require realistic fictional legislative process, constitutional/civil-rights protections, evidence, legal review and balanced perspectives. No mechanic that strips minority rights or targets groups based on faith.

## Halal business, ads, zakat and nonprofits
- Ethical shops, delivery, teaching, services, fair trade, employment, receipts, customer rights and transparent prices. Avoid interest-bearing debt products, gambling, prohibited goods, deceptive ads, exploitative monetization.
- Advertising: clearly labeled optional in-world sponsorship at ordinary commercial venues; no ads on Qur'an text, prayer interfaces, mosque worship or pilgrimage rites; contextual not behavioral, no tracking of religious practices, parental controls, frequency caps and community approval.
- Zakat: optional educational calculator with nisab/haul, assets/debts, recipient eligibility and school-of-law differences; **not** automatic real-world religious determination. Qur'an 9:60 reference lead. Real-money giving only through vetted nonprofit payment providers with clear consent, fees, receipts, refund policy and local legal compliance; never imply the game itself is a registered charity.
- Sadaqah and nonprofit missions: food distribution, clinics, education, water, accessibility; fictional donation uses separate currency and clearly labeled simulation. No pay-to-win, purchasable blessings, donor prestige or financial pressure.
- Sponsor disclosures, advertiser eligibility policy, sanctions/AML checks where applicable, charity due diligence, child protection and auditing required before real payments.

## Travel and airport scenarios
Ticket booking, luggage limits, boarding, transit, city arrival, passport/visa learning (fictional documents), prayer spaces, halal food, accessibility and safe driving. Optional airport-security scenario may portray unjust extra screening, but must avoid profiling as inevitable or universal. Branches: calm cooperation, request supervisor, document incident safely, obtain legal aid, submit complaint, learn jurisdiction-specific rights; no illegal evasion, false travel advice or private identity collection.

## Mosques and landmark atlas
Notable mosque discovery map, architectural history, accessibility, prayer facilities, local guides, respectful educational tours and licensed imagery. Candidates: Masjid al-Haram, Prophet's Mosque, Al-Aqsa compound, Sultan Ahmed Mosque, Hassan II Mosque, Great Mosque of Djenné, Faisal Mosque, Istiqlal Mosque, National Mosque Abuja. Confirm names, permissions, political sensitivities, mapping licenses and local context before publication. Do not imply access to restricted areas or copy real interiors without rights.
