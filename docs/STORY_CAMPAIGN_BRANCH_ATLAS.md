# Dunya — branching campaign atlas
Status: narrative requirements only. No game runtime or validated religious ruling.

## Chapter connection rules
Each story must define: prerequisites, player/character eligibility, city, NPC motivations, evidence leads, 3 meaningful peaceful choices, one repair path, an accessibility route, consequence scope, re-entry conditions, and post-story institutional change. Main campaign chapters are not mandatory prerequisites for family/grief arcs.

## Story chains
### Chain 1 — Truth and Dawah
Ragebait platform -> false rumor about mosque -> player verifies sources (Qur'an 49:6 research lead) -> listening-first street dawah -> independent journalism -> privacy-first alternative platform. Bad branch: player shares unverified claim; consequence is loss of fictional trust and a correction opportunity, not divine sin score. Player can exit confrontation safely.

### Chain 2 — Food, Eid and Mutual Aid
Food conglomerate pressure -> supplier welfare audit -> halal butchery educational oversight -> Eid food distribution -> family food security cooperative. Require trained professional handling, local food law and scholar-reviewed ritual content. No graphic slaughter minigame.

### Chain 3 — Family, Death and Legacy
Nikah (optional) -> household -> caregiving -> serious illness (non-deterministic) -> optional janazah -> estate documentation -> heirs and probate review -> next generation. No automatic asset transfer, forced grief, or divine punishment for illness. Alternative unmarried/childless family routes.

### Chain 4 — Interfaith Protection and Civic Reform
Threat to mosque/church/synagogue -> emergency services and safe evacuation -> lawful reporting -> trauma-sensitive rebuilding -> multi-faith civic safety policy. No vigilante combat or presumption of guilt based on identity.

### Chain 5 — Detention, Faith and Resettlement
Fictional legally eligible noncitizen -> detention -> request religious accommodations -> counsel and legal remedies -> release/transfer/contested removal -> reviewed destination city pack -> rebuilding. Never randomly select a Muslim-majority country without lawful narrative basis.

### Chain 6 — Privacy and Trade
Invasive data broker -> informed consent campaign -> local private inference -> fair-trade supplier registry -> transparent procurement -> independent security and labor audit. No offensive hacking or evasion guidance.

## Cross-chain consistency tests (specification)
- A character can decline marriage, parenting, public debate and detention storylines without losing the main campaign.
- The player cannot be punished for being attacked or discriminated against.
- Consent withdrawn during dawah immediately ends conversation without failure.
- Death and janazah never imply estate is settled; debts, heirs and legal review intervene.
- A religious rule changed by scholarly review invalidates dependent content approvals.
- City transitions preserve only lawful, narratively plausible belongings and relationships.
- An NPC's faith, ethnicity, occupation or clothing never determines guilt.
- Real political organizations are not fictional conspirators without substantiated source-based depiction.
