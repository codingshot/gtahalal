# Janazah, burial and grief — educational story requirements
Status: proposed educational simulation, not a ritual instruction certified by scholars. Non-graphic, optional, grief-sensitive.

## Chapters and player activities
1. News of death: compassionate communication, confirm documentation, contact family and mosque/funeral professionals, respect privacy.
2. Immediate arrangements: identify authorized decision makers, local death certification, transportation and funeral-provider requirements; no assumption every jurisdiction is identical.
3. Preparation of deceased: learn about ghusl of deceased, kafan, dignity, privacy, qualified same-gender or permitted kinship arrangements, and local legal/health constraints. Use non-graphic diagrams and supervised professional demonstration, never body manipulation as arcade play.
4. Salat al-Janazah: congregation etiquette, intention, standing, takbirs, supplication and salams; exact sequences and recitations vary by school, so provide reviewed school-specific guided lesson and no speed scoring. Qur'an/hadith leads must be independently verified.
5. Cemetery and burial: lawful site and permits, grave orientation and depth handled by licensed professionals, respectful procession, accessibility, no vandalism or sensationalism.
6. Mourning: condolences, supporting bereaved households, food/help, grief resources, children asking questions, privacy and culturally sensitive practices.
7. Funeral economy: compare transparent fees, avoid pressure and unnecessary expenses, organize community support without financial coercion.
8. Continuity: death record triggers estate-administration story, kinship changes and optional next-generation arc, never an instant inheritance payout.

## Narrative dilemmas
Unnecessary funeral upselling, pressure to disclose private medical details, relatives disagreeing on arrangements, accessibility obstacles, unpaid funeral bills, a grieving child seeking reassurance, an absent relative returning. Resolve through compassion, lawful procedure and mediation.

## Acceptance
Professional and scholarly review; non-graphic; optional skip and grief support; no points for death; consent/privacy; accessible alternative to cemetery navigation; no unreviewed ritual instructions released.
