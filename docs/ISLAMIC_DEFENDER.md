# Islamic Defender — Street Dawah and Nonviolent Self-Protection
Status: requirements, not implemented. Qualified scholarly and legal review required.

## Purpose
Train respectful, evidence-based dawah, listening, honest responses, emotional regulation, conflict de-escalation, safety and nonviolent self-protection. Not a combat or coercive proselytization game. Player may always leave, seek help, or end a discussion.

## Learning chapters
1. Niyyah and sincerity — private reflection; no piety points (Sahih al-Bukhari 1).
2. Wisdom, good instruction and respectful discussion (Qur'an 16:125).
3. No compulsion in religion (Qur'an 2:256).
4. Common questions: tawhid, prophethood, Qur'an, worship, women and family, ethics, other faiths, history, science, suffering, misconceptions. Include accurate alternative views and uncertainty; never invent quotations or pressure an NPC to convert.
5. Listen, clarify, ask permission to share, identify assumptions, distinguish fact from opinion and disputed interpretations.
6. Respond calmly to mockery and insults (Qur'an 25:63; 41:34).
7. Safe public table: permit/venue rules, accessible placement, consent-first dialogue, privacy and safeguarding.
8. De-escalation: keep distance, disengage, seek bystander or emergency assistance, avoid pursuit or retaliation.
9. Self-protection: physical force only as a last resort when an immediate threat cannot be avoided, limited to necessity and proportionality, stop when danger ends; no offensive attacks, weapons or glorified violence. Legal rules differ by jurisdiction and require separate review.
10. Post-encounter reflection: harm repair, apology where appropriate, evidence review and retry.

## Game over and feedback
- Deliberate unjustified attack on a person: immediately pause; **HARAM — Unjustified Harm** only if juristically reviewed; link evidence and restart scenario. Qur'an 5:32 (sanctity of life) and 2:190 (no transgression) are source leads; not blanket rulings for every event.
- Harm to women or children: no special permission to harm adult men; **all innocent people protected**. A deliberate attack on any nonthreatening NPC ends scenario; do not make women or children fight targets. Any age/gender-specific text must be accurate and reviewed.
- Trees and animals: intentional needless destruction or cruelty triggers **Harm to Creation** and restart; Qur'an 7:56 (avoid corruption on earth) is general principle; hadith on planting (Bukhari 2320) is supportive but not a literal legal rule for every damaged leaf.
- Accidental bump/collision: contextual warning and repair opportunity; do not label every mistake haram.
- Threat ended but player keeps striking: **Excessive Force — Stop** and fail. Use 2:190 as review lead, with separate legal/fiqh review of context.
- Player loses consciousness: **Scenario Ended — Seek Safety**, not 'haram' or punishment for being attacked.
- UI buttons: View Qur'an, View Hadith, Why This Applies, Differences/Context, Retry, Accessibility Alternative.
- Never represent divine verdict, quantify sins, or claim to issue fatwas.

## Acceptance criteria
- No mission awards points for violence, humiliating non-Muslims or converting NPCs.
- Dialogue always supports respectful disagreement and exit.
- Threat simulation favors escape and help; no fighting unless scripted immediate attack with safe last-resort defense.
- Automated test matrix: unprovoked attack, accidental collision, intentional tree damage, bystander injury, attacker retreats, user disengages, ambiguous threat, child NPC, NPC declines dawah, source missing/unapproved.
- Only approved rules may render definitive religious labels; unreviewed cases show neutral safety feedback.
- Accessibility: no violence simulation required to complete learning, captions, keyboard/gamepad/touch, reduced motion and skip option.
