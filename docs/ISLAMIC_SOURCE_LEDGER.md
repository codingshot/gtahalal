## Source quality taxonomy
- Verified text: reference and page content inspected.
- Context reviewed: adjacent text, tafsir or hadith variants assessed.
- Interpretation reviewed: qualified scholar has assessed application.
- Approved for release: scholarly, legal/cultural and licensing gates complete.
Never collapse these four states into a single 'verified' boolean.

## Copyright
Prefer linking to Quran.com and Sunnah.com; translation, tafsir, audio and recitation rights require explicit permission or suitable license before redistribution. Do not scrape entire corpora.
