# Dunya — Generations Saga: lifecycle and family world bible
Status: proposed requirements only. No runtime logic. Religious, legal, cultural and trauma-sensitive review required.

## Narrative concept
Ahmed and Fatimah and future playable family members experience childhood, education, adult responsibilities, marriage, parenting, aging, loss, inheritance and generational legacy. Shaytan is an unseen theological temptation, not a physically rendered character or proof of a real-world conspiracy. Industry antagonists are fictional systems incentivizing neglect, greed, disinformation or exploitation. Players resist by learning, caring, acting fairly and building institutions. No assumption that every player marries, has children or outlives loved ones.

## Core arcs
1. Family formation: adult consent, compatibility, nikah, mahr, witnesses and civil registration with fiqh/jurisdiction review.
2. Shared household: mutually agreed budgeting, privacy, work, communication, in-laws, conflict resolution and protection from abuse.
3. New child: prenatal/postnatal care, safeguarding, naming, care and culturally reviewed optional religious practices. No prescriptive medical advice.
4. Childhood: nurturing, safe play, school, Qur'an literacy, respect, disability inclusion, protection from bullying and digital harms.
5. Adolescence: growing agency, mentorship, identity, peer pressure, social media literacy, safe boundaries and responsible choices. No sexualization of minors.
6. Adult children: education, careers, independence, parents' needs, ethical trade and maintaining kinship without coercion.
7. Elder care: accessible homes, mobility, medical referrals, finances, consent, dementia-sensitive care, respite for caregivers.
8. Serious illness and death: advance planning, respectful family support, lawful documentation, funeral coordination, grief.
9. Janazah and burial: preparation, funeral prayer, cemetery logistics, interment and bereavement, with qualified mosque/funeral professionals.
10. Estate and inheritance: assets, debts, valid bequests, heirs, disputes, rights of minors, and civil probate.
11. Legacy: next generation inherits relationships, institutions, consequences and stories; avoid presenting spiritual merit as a numeric transferable asset.

## Industry-pressure pairings
Marriage: exploitative matchmaking, deceptive fees -> transparent introductions and safeguarding.
Parenting: addictive child-targeted media -> age-appropriate privacy-preserving learning.
Education: misinformation -> evidence-based curricula and teacher support.
Food: unsafe marketing -> healthy affordable family supply.
Finance: predatory debt -> clear family budgeting and Sharia-reviewed financing.
Housing: inaccessible rentals -> safe multigenerational homes.
Caregiving: exploitative services -> transparent, dignified support networks.
Funerals: predatory funeral pricing -> dignified affordable burial cooperative.
Inheritance: document fraud, unequal access -> lawful estate transparency and independent mediation.
All opponents fictional, with nuanced NPCs and nonviolent alternatives.

## Continuity model (specification)
Family relationships, kinship graph, household membership, ages, guardianship, residence, education, property title, debts, lawful marriage status, dependent needs, care duties, death record, funeral status, estate administration, inheritance allocation, and next-generation unlocks. Preserve consent, privacy, jurisdiction and legal validity. Never assume one person's marriage grants control over another person's autonomy.

## Cross-cutting safeguards
Optional grief content and skip mode; no forced child loss, death or marriage for progress. No real user family data or genealogical identifiers. No religious scoring of suffering, miscarriage, illness or bereavement. Diverse family structures and disability inclusion. No punitive gender stereotypes. Qualified reviewers needed for disputed fiqh.
