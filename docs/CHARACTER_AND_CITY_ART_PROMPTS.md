## Negative guidance
Avoid cartoon proportions, uncanny faces, inconsistent identity between views, anatomical errors, transparent or implausibly tight modest garments, generic fantasy architecture, copied game characters, real logos, weapons, oversexualization, stereotypes, AI text gibberish, and unlicensed sacred interiors.

## Mockup-to-asset process
For each user-provided reference: preserve exact composition first, write an observed shot breakdown, generate original concept variants, get approval, create 3D model and material pass, compare same camera and lighting, then optimize. Do not invent mockup details when unavailable.
