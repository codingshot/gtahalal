# Dunya — scenario authoring standard v1
Status: requirements only. No runtime code, no approved fatwa, no personal data.

## Every scenario must have
- Stable story ID and revision, genre, city, time, character eligibility, age suitability and content warnings.
- Logline, specific player objective, NPC goals, optional entry/skip, preconditions, exact branching choices and peaceful exit.
- Consequences: community impact, trust, resources, relationships and institutions; **never** divine reward, salvation, or conversion score.
- Failure/repair: proportional consequence, apology/correction path, no shame for being a victim, sick, bereaved or disabled.
- Faith claims: atomic claim IDs, Quran.com/Sunnah.com canonical references, quote rights, textual verification, contextual review, jurisprudence profile, qualified reviewer, release approval. Mark every unsupported claim pending.
- Nonreligious claims: dated legal/medical/technical source, jurisdiction, subject-matter reviewer and expiration trigger.
- Technical contract (future only): triggers, state dependencies, replay/idempotency expectations, saved state, accessibility alternatives, localization, performance budget and test fixtures.
- Approval status separately for narrative, religious, legal, cultural, safety, assets and implementation.

## Story beat template
1. Hook — ordinary situation with credible stakes.
2. Investigation — hear competing accounts and verify evidence.
3. Dilemma — at least three meaningful nonviolent options, including disengagement where appropriate.
4. Action — player builds, helps, negotiates, learns or advocates.
5. Outcome — measurable fictional community effects.
6. Repair — a mistaken decision can be corrected.
7. Reflection — optional private learning, with evidence links.
8. Connection — explicit next chapter or self-contained ending.

## Mandatory audit questions
- Does a character have agency and dignity even when disagreeing with the protagonist?
- Does the story stereotype a faith, ethnicity, profession or city?
- Does a single verse get used as a blanket rule beyond its context?
- Is a criminal/legal claim presented as settled without jurisdiction/date?
- Is the player punished for an event beyond their control?
- Can a disabled player or a player opting out of grief/violence still finish?
- Is there a path to accountability without revenge?
- Can Claude point to exact files and acceptance tests for every branch?

## Definition of documentation-ready
All fields present, unresolved claims explicitly pending, two independent story reviews, safety and accessibility checklist completed, source ledger cross-linked by ID. Documentation-ready is NOT release-approved.
