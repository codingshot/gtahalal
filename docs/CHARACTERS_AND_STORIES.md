# Characters, gender-specific settings and story modes

## Main protagonists
**Ahmed:** playable male protagonist; community volunteer, ethical entrepreneur and student of knowledge. Starting story: help a neighbor, learn Arabic, manage responsibilities, serve community.
**Fatimah:** playable female protagonist wearing hijab by default; educator, entrepreneur and community organizer. Starting story: teach literacy, support healthcare access, organize community services and mentor others. Do not restrict her to domestic or passive roles.

Character models, voices and personal histories must be original. Do not represent either as an actual user. Customization supports diverse skin tones, cultures, disability aids, modest attire, accessible equipment and respectful body animations. Name transliteration: use **Fatimah** as default; confirm if founder prefers **Fartinah**.

## Gender-specific design requirements
- Offer appropriate male/female prayer spaces and modest clothing; model context-dependent boundaries and interactions with consultation, without humiliation, surveillance or piety scoring.
- Story access based on character and approved jurisprudential settings only when materially relevant. Equal access to general city, learning, civic, career, service and economic systems.
- No romance/sexual gameplay, objectification or harassment; respectful interactions for all NPCs.
- Optional local scholarly guidance for areas with differing opinions; document school/region-specific interpretations and avoid claiming universal consensus.
- Privacy-first profiles, no collection of real religious observance or gender identity data.

## Story mode approval
Every story has a narrative brief, character eligibility, intended learning outcome, source references, source authenticity/context, age rating, local/cultural review, jurisprudential review, content warning if needed, accessibility alternative, acceptance tests and a publish flag. Only approved stories appear in release builds.

## Approved story candidates
Everyday Dunya, Community Builder, Student of Knowledge, Ethical Entrepreneur, Ramadan Service, Pilgrimage Learning, Traveler, Family and Neighbors, Accessibility Champion, Environment Steward, Cooperative Service.

## Path to Imam (educational fiction, not certification)
Prerequisites: Ahmed opts into supervised knowledge route. Chapters: intention and adab; Arabic reading; memorization with qualified teacher; tajwid; purification and salah basics; fiqh differences; khutbah preparation and ethics; pastoral care; conflict mediation; mosque operations; supervised leading of congregational prayer in permitted fictional scenarios. Assessment: knowledge checks and NPC mentor feedback, never actual religious authority, fatwa permission or divine approval. Qualified scholars approve curriculum; show local mosque qualification varies.

## Path to Scholar / Community Educator
Fatimah and other eligible characters can follow Arabic, Qur'an studies, tajwid, teaching, service, mentoring, ethics, women scholars in Islamic history and educational leadership. Do not imply that this is a substitute for recognized Islamic education.

## Branching narrative requirements
Dialogue choices affect fictional trust, relationships and community outcomes; no divine reward counter. Niyyah prompt optional and private. Provide no-shame retries, peaceful conflict resolution and alternate paths for disability and accessibility.
