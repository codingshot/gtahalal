# Dunya world and city pack specification

## Candidate cities
| City | Mission opportunities | Cultural review |
|---|---|---|
| Fictional Miami-inspired starter | mosque community, food bank, neighborhood service | local reviewers |
| Nairobi | ethical commerce, community gardening, accessibility, waste reduction | Kenyan Muslim reviewers |
| Lagos | transport safety, flood response, fair trade, public cleanliness | Nigerian Muslim reviewers |
| Addis Ababa | language learning, heritage, markets, mutual aid | Ethiopian Muslim reviewers |
| Cairo | historic preservation, pedestrian access, education | Egyptian reviewers |
| Istanbul | waqf heritage, commerce, hospitality | Turkish reviewers |
| Dakar | coastal cleanup, craft and community | Senegalese reviewers |
| Karachi | water stewardship, neighborhood health | Pakistani reviewers |
| Dhaka | flooding, recycling, accessible routes | Bangladeshi reviewers |
| Jakarta | flood resilience, transit, community kitchens | Indonesian reviewers |
| Sarajevo | heritage and intercommunity service | Bosnian reviewers |
| Mogadishu | education, entrepreneurship, civic rebuilding | Somali reviewers |
| Makkah and Madinah | respectful educational pilgrimage/history only | specialist religious and cultural review |

No city is defined only by hardship. Missions are fictional proposals, not verified neighborhood conditions. Use local co-authors, consent for identifiable landmarks, no sacred-site arcade mechanics, and avoid geopolitical or sectarian stereotypes.

## City pack schema
id, displayName, locale, climate, districts, travel, navmesh, spawnPoints, npcArchetypes, missionIds, originalAssetManifest, licenseLedger, performanceBudget, culturalReviewerIds, reviewStatus, approvedModes. Pack loads on demand, supports offline caching, accessibility and RTL. No location surveillance.

## City acceptance
Original visual identity, local review, accurate cultural context, licensed assets, 30 FPS baseline target on defined hardware, complete navigation and collision QA, no privacy leaks.
