# Family estates, faraid, wills and lineage — requirements
Status: educational simulation only; not legal, tax or fatwa advice.

## Estate process
Death certification -> asset inventory -> ownership verification -> funeral expenses and other priority obligations subject to local law and fiqh -> valid debts -> review of bequests -> determination of heirs and impediments -> school/jurisdiction-specific faraid allocation -> estate accounting -> dispute resolution -> title transfer -> safeguarding minors and dependents. Never hardcode percentages before verifying heir constellation and applicable legal framework.

## Gameplay proposals
Find documents, reconcile bank/land records, identify hidden liabilities without hacking, prevent document fraud, resolve family disagreements through independent mediation, protect widows/orphans, ensure women and eligible relatives receive lawful shares, administer trust/waqf and support surviving dependents. Fraud and theft are antagonist actions, not player objectives.

## Lineage and child safeguarding
Kinship graph represents fictional people only; no real user genealogies, DNA, adoption status or identity documents.
