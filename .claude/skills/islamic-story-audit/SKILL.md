# Dunya story audit

## Inputs
One story spec, its branching outline, cited source ledger, applicable city/era and intended protagonist. Read CLAUDE.md and the Quran/Sunnah skills first.

## Audit procedure
1. Identify scene goal, stakes, character eligibility, agency, choices, branches, consequences and exit.
2. Trace every normative claim to evidence records; distinguish religious teaching, legal fact, fictional plot and game rule.
3. Check continuity: prerequisites, timeline, inventory, location, character status, wudu certainty, marriage state, legal status, outcome persistence and impossible transitions.
4. Check ethics: no coerced conversion, profiling, unsupported real-organization allegations, victim-blaming, vigilantism, discrimination or divine reward scoring.
5. Check choices: at least one peaceful exit, meaningful agency, accessibility alternative, no unavoidable failure caused by identity, no humiliation.
6. Check legal and cultural context: immigration, marriage, animal welfare, permits and civil rights require location- and date-specific reviewers.
7. Assign severity: blocker, major, minor, editorial. Mark evidence status separately from design completeness.
8. Produce a branch table and numbered findings with exact spec file, claim, source, defect, suggested rewrite, owner/reviewer type and acceptance test.
9. Do not change gameplay logic, invent facts or approve religious rulings. Only update docs/issues within authorized scope.

## Required report
Story ID; branch coverage; citation coverage; unsupported claims; harmful outcomes; continuity defects; proposed fixes; review blockers; implementation-ready yes/no.
